## Ingredients : 
- 2 egg
- 3 Green chill
- 1 onion 
- 0.25 tea spoon of Turmeric powder
- 3 table spoon cooking oil (soybean or butter)

## Pseudo code  
1. Get a bowl
2. 3 well chopped green chills
3. 1 well chopped Onion 
4. Give 0.25 tea spoon of Turmeric powder
5. Crack the two eggs
6. Mix well by hand or spoon 
7. Give 3 table spoon of soybean oil in the pan
8. Hit it until it we see white smokes
9. Then give the mix on the pan
10. Fry both side of the egg

### And All done
