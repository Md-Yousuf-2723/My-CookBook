## Ingredients :

- 3 Borboti (Yardlong beans) - well chopped into small pieces
- 2 Potol (Pointed gourds) - well chopped into small pieces
- 3 onions - well chopped
- 3 cloves of garlic
- 6–7 green chillies - well chopped
- Ginger - a piece about the size of tip of afinger
- 3 table spoon cooking oil (soybean oil)
- 1 tea spoon kalon jira (nigella seeds)
- Salt - a little more than 0.5 tea spoon
- 0.5 tea spoon turmeric powder
- 0.25 tea spoon coriander powder
- 1 egg
- 1 Pua (250g rice) (slightly less) cooked rice
- 0.5 tea spoon salt (for the rice)

## Pseudo code

1. Get all the vegetables.
2. Finely chop the 3 Borboti.
3. Finely chop the 2 Potol.
4. Finely chop the 3 onions.
5. Chop the 3 cloves of garlic.
6. Chop the 6–7 green chillies.
7. Chop the ginger into small pieces.
8. Wash all the vegetables properly.
9. Heat the korai (wok).
10. Give 3 table spoon of soybean oil into the korai.
11. Give 1 tea spoon of kalon jira.
12. Wait until you hear the immediate "fut-fut" sound.
13. Then give all the chopped vegetables into the korai.
14. Stir with a khunti (spatula) for 5 seconds.
15. Stir the vegetables again.
16. Give a little more than 0.5 tea spoon of salt.
17. Cook the vegetables until they become soft.
18. Give 0.5 tea spoon of turmeric powder.
19. Give 0.25 tea spoon of coriander powder.
20. Stir again with the khunti.
21. Crack 1 egg into the korai.
22. Cook while stirring so that the food does not stick to the korai.
23. Then gradually add slightly less than 1 Pua of cooked rice.
24. Give another 0.5 tea spoon of salt.
25. Mix everything well.
26. Cook on medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally.

### And All done! 🍚🔥
